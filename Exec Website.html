why I'm running

I joined SecSoc for the CTFs and stayed for the people who'd explain a exploit at 1am because they wanted to, not because they had to.

That's the version of the club I want more people to find earlier. Too many members hear about workshops after they've happened, sponsors after the free swag is gone, and the Discord after the interesting conversation has moved on. As VP, my job is the plumbing that makes sure none of that keeps happening — so the exec can spend its energy on the club itself, not on keeping it visible.

platform

01

A term calendar members can actually plan around

One shared calendar for CTFs, workshops, socials, and sponsor sessions, published at the start of term instead of trickling out week to week. Fewer clashes, fewer members finding out an event happened after it did.

02

Sponsor relationships that outlast one exec team

Turn one-off sponsor pizza nights into a standing pipeline — recurring workshops, CTF sponsorship, and a referral path into internships, handed off cleanly to whoever runs VP next.

03

A real on-ramp for first-years

A standing beginner track alongside the advanced CTF stream, so someone who's never touched a terminal has something to do at a meeting besides watch.
